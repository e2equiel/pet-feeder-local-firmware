Private backups

Two matching full backups come first. Then the installer activates a reversible overlay and verifies the local API. It preserves saved meals and never serves food. Recovery runs automatically if verification fails.

Backups and a private Home Assistant setup file will be saved in your computer's Documents / PetFeederLocalBackups folder, unless you selected another folder when starting the installer.

Experimental: tested on one exact hardware profile. Observe actual meals before relying on it. Battery feeding and clock retention after complete power loss remain unverified.

Keep the feeder powered on

The backup can take several minutes. Leave this window and the installer running until verification finishes.
